Our Story

Hospitality, crafted with care.

Ruby Elizabeth Events is a Southern event company devoted to thoughtful hospitality. We believe a celebration is more than a gathering—it is an evening of soft light, shared laughter, and the quiet care that turns a day into a memory.

Our work centers on the details that make a moment feel complete. Uplighting that settles gently across the chapel and grove. A photobooth that gathers joy into keepsakes. Floral design that honors natural beauty with Southern grace and careful craft.

Based in Eagleville, we bring that same care to weddings and events at venues across Middle Tennessee, within 45 miles—from first glow to final bloom.

The Sisters

Two sisters. One standard of gracious service.

Ruby Elizabeth Events is owned and run by two sisters, one of them a 7th grader at The Webb School, who share a love for beautiful celebrations. Their work is defined by warmth, intention, and Southern grace, with calm attention to every detail.

Together they offer event uplighting, a photobooth experience, and fresh floral design for weddings and events at venues across Middle Tennessee. We keep our prices affordable so more of your budget goes toward enhancing your guests' experience, and we donate a portion of all revenue to doing good in our community.
